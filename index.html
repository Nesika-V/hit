Questions

What is an attack surface?

Everything about your organization that someone on the internet can reach: websites, subdomains, servers, open ports, certificates and cloud storage.

What does attack surface management do?

It finds those assets, checks what is exposed and keeps the list current, so a forgotten server does not become the way in.

Why do forgotten assets matter?

Attackers scan the internet for old staging hosts, unpatched services and public storage buckets. If your team does not know an asset exists, nobody is patching it.
